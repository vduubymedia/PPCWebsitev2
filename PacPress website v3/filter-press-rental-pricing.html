Filter Press Rental Pricing

Rental pricing depends on press size, rental duration, freight, support equipment, and application requirements. The rates below are monthly starting points and may vary by project.

Direct Answer

PacPress rental filter press pricing starts at $500/month for a Lab Press and ranges up to $12,800/month for a 1200mm press. Final pricing depends on press size, rental duration, freight, support equipment such as pumps and tanks, and application-specific requirements. [PLACEHOLDER: Confirm rental pricing table]

What Changes the Final Price

Press Size

Larger cubic foot capacity generally costs more per month than smaller presses.

Rental Duration

Rental length can affect the rate compared to the monthly starting point.

Freight

Shipping the press and any support equipment to your site is priced separately.

Pumps, Tanks & Hoses

Support equipment may be added depending on your site and application needs.

Setup & Support

Site-specific setup assistance may affect the total project cost.

Application Requirements

Specialized materials or discharge requirements can affect configuration and cost.

Rental Credit Toward Purchase

PacPress is the manufacturer of the equipment you rent. In many cases, rental payments may be applied toward the purchase of a new filter press. [PLACEHOLDER: Confirm whether rental credit toward purchase is always available or case-by-case]
