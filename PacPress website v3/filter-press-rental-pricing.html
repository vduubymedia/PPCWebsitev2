Filter Press Rental Pricing

Rental pricing depends on press capacity, rental duration, freight, support equipment, and application requirements. PacPress prepares a quote around the actual jobsite and process.

Request a Quote Sizing Guide
Direct Answer

Filter press rental pricing is project-specific. PacPress uses the required press capacity, rental term, delivery location, support equipment, and site conditions to prepare a quote for the complete rental configuration.

What Your Rental Quote Accounts For

Quote ComponentWhat PacPress Evaluates
Press capacityFiltration volume, cycle requirements, and material characteristics.
Rental termExpected project duration and equipment availability.
FreightDelivery location, access, and return transportation.
Support equipmentPumps, tanks, hoses, controls, and connections required for the application.
Site requirementsSetup, power, cake handling, and application-specific support.

Your written quote identifies the rental configuration, included equipment, freight assumptions, term, and project-specific costs.

What Changes the Final Price

Press Capacity

Larger-capacity presses and more demanding cycle requirements can affect the quoted rate.

Rental Duration

The expected project duration is one of the factors used to prepare the rental quote.

Freight

Shipping the press and any support equipment to your site is priced separately.

Pumps, Tanks & Hoses

Support equipment may be added depending on your site and application needs.

Setup & Support

Site-specific setup assistance may affect the total project cost.

Application Requirements

Specialized materials or discharge requirements can affect configuration and cost.

From Rental to Permanent Equipment

PacPress manufactures filter presses as well as supporting temporary projects. If your need becomes permanent, ask the sales team about equipment-purchase options and the terms available for your project.
