Direct Answer

PacPress rents filter presses that separate solids from liquids for temporary, project-based dewatering needs. Why Companies Rent Instead of Buy

01

Emergency Capacity

Equipment failure, unplanned downtime, or an urgent cleanup that can’t wait for a capital purchase.

02

Seasonal Demand

Production spikes, harvest runs, or rainy-season overflow that only last part of the year.

03

Project-Based Work

Remediation, dredging, and cleanout projects with a defined start and end date.

04

Maintenance Downtime

Bridging the gap while permanent dewatering equipment is repaired or replaced.

05

Pilot Testing

Proving out dewatering performance on real material before committing to a permanent system.

06

Avoiding Capital Purchase

Getting solids separation capacity without the upfront cost of owned equipment.
