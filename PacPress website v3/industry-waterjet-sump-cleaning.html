Common Rental Applications

Typical ways PacPress rental filter presses are used in waterjet sump cleaning.

Garnet Dewatering

Separating spent garnet abrasive from sump water for disposal or further handling.

Metal Fines Removal

Removing metal fines and cutting residue accumulated in sump tanks.

Sump Volume Reduction

Reducing sludge volume ahead of hauling or disposal.

Shop Downtime Reduction

Speeding up cleanout turnaround to get cutting operations back online faster.

Periodic Maintenance Cleanouts

Supporting scheduled or as-needed sump maintenance programs.

When Renting Makes Sense

01

Scheduled sump cleanouts

Periodic maintenance requires the sump to be cleaned and garnet/fines removed.

02

Minimizing shop downtime

Faster on-site dewatering shortens the time cutting operations are paused for cleanout.

03

Disposal volume reduction

Dewatered garnet and fines are cheaper and easier to haul than wet sludge.

04

Small-to-mid rental capacity

Sump cleanouts typically fit smaller rental press sizes rather than large-capacity systems.
