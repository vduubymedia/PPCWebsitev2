Common Rental Applications

Typical ways PacPress rental filter presses are used in industrial and petrochemical.

Turnaround Process Water

Managing process water solids generated during scheduled plant turnarounds.

Maintenance Outage Support

Providing temporary dewatering while permanent plant equipment is serviced.

When Renting Makes Sense

01

Plant turnarounds

Scheduled turnarounds generate temporary process water volumes that need short-term dewatering support.

02

Maintenance outages

Permanent dewatering equipment is offline for planned or unplanned maintenance.

03

Operational continuity

Production needs to continue while a permanent solution is repaired, replaced, or sized for future capacity.

04

Temporary process needs

A short-term process change requires solids separation capacity that doesn’t justify a capital purchase.
