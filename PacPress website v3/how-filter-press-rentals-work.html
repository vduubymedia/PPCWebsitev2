The Rental Process

How Filter Press Rentals Work

From first call to on-site operation, here is what to expect when you rent a filter press from PacPress.

Request a Rental Quote Sizing Guide
Direct Answer

A PacPress filter press rental starts with the application and site details needed for sizing. The written quote defines the equipment, rental term, freight, support, and delivery schedule. End-of-rental options are handled according to the rental agreement.

When a Rental Is Appropriate

01

Emergency Need

Equipment failure or an urgent cleanup that can’t wait for a capital purchase.

02

Temporary Capacity

A defined period where more dewatering capacity is needed than you currently have.

03

Project-Based Work

Remediation, dredging, or cleanout projects with a defined start and end date.

04

Seasonal Demand

Production spikes or rainy-season overflow tied to a specific time of year.

05

Maintenance Downtime

Bridging the gap while permanent equipment is serviced or replaced.

06

Pilot Testing

Proving out performance on your material before a permanent purchase decision.

Step-by-Step Rental Process

1. Share Project Details

Tell PacPress your material or slurry type, volume, timeline, and site location.

2. PacPress Sizes and Quotes the Rental

A specialist recommends a press size and any needed support equipment, and provides pricing.

3. Freight and Site Prep Are Coordinated

PacPress coordinates freight timing; you prepare site access, power, and space for setup and cake handling.

4. Equipment Arrives and Is Set Up

Delivery timing is confirmed after equipment availability, freight, site access, and setup requirements are reviewed.

5. You Operate With Factory Support

Technical-support options and responsibilities are reviewed during quoting and documented for the rental.

6. Return, Extend, or Discuss Purchase Credit

At the end of the rental, return the equipment, extend the term, or ask about applying rental payments toward a purchase.

Timeline and Logistics Notes

Actual timelines depend on equipment availability, freight distance, and how much support equipment your project needs. Sites with straightforward access and available utilities typically move faster than remote or constrained sites.

Discuss your specific timeline with a PacPress specialist early — the sooner project details are shared, the sooner freight and setup can be scheduled.

Process FAQ

A rental is a good fit for emergency needs, temporary capacity, project-based work, seasonal demand, maintenance downtime, or pilot testing before a purchase decision.

Delivery timing depends on equipment availability, project location, freight coordination, and site readiness. PacPress confirms the schedule during the quote process.

Site needs vary by project, but generally include space for setup, access for delivery, a power source, and a plan for discharge or hauling of filter cake. PacPress can help confirm site requirements during the quote process.

Available technical-support options and responsibilities are reviewed during the quote process and documented for the rental.

End-of-rental options are handled under the rental agreement. Contact PacPress to discuss an extension or a separate equipment purchase.
