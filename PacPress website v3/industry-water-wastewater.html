Plant Maintenance and Capacity Limits

Municipal and industrial wastewater plants rely on dewatering equipment to manage sludge volume. When that equipment goes down for maintenance, or when lagoon storage reaches its limit, sludge can back up quickly and create operational and compliance risk.

A rental filter press provides temporary dewatering capacity so a plant can keep operating while permanent equipment is serviced, replaced, or expanded.

When Renting Makes Sense

01

Planned or emergency maintenance

Permanent dewatering equipment is offline for service, repair, or replacement.

02

Lagoon capacity limits

Storage lagoons are reaching capacity and sludge needs to be dewatered and removed.

03

Pilot testing

Evaluating filter press performance on plant sludge before a capital equipment decision.

04

Emergency response

An unplanned event requires fast, temporary dewatering capacity to keep the plant operating.

What We Need From You

Information Needed to Size and Quote This Rental

Share what you know — PacPress can help fill in gaps during the quote conversation.

  • Material or slurry type — what is being dewatered or clarified.
  • Flow rate or volume — how much material needs processing, and how often.
  • Solids percentage — if known, helps size press capacity and cycle time.
  • Desired cake dryness — how dry the discharged solids need to be for handling or disposal.
  • Project timeline — expected start date and rental duration.
  • Site location — for freight planning and site-specific requirements.
  • Available utilities — power and water access on site.
  • Support equipment needs — whether pumps, tanks, or hoses are needed alongside the press.
  • Discharge or reuse goal — where clarified water or filter cake will go after processing.
