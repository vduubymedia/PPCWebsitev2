Simple Explanation

What a Rental Filter Press Actually Does

A rental filter press gives a site temporary solids separation without committing to a permanent installation. It is useful when water needs to be clarified, solids need to be collected, or sludge needs to be dewatered quickly.

It separates solids from liquid.

Dirty water, slurry, sludge, or process waste is pumped into the press. Filter plates capture solids while clarified water exits the system for reuse, discharge, or further treatment.

It turns wet waste into manageable cake.

Instead of hauling large volumes of watery sludge, a filter press helps reduce liquid volume and creates filter cake that can be handled, stored, tested, or disposed.
