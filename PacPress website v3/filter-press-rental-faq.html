Direct Answer

PacPress rents filter presses for temporary and project-based dewatering. Equipment capacity, pricing, delivery timing, freight, and support equipment are confirmed for the application during the quote process.

Rental Fit

Renting typically makes sense for emergency equipment failure, temporary or seasonal capacity needs, project-based work with a defined timeline, maintenance downtime, or pilot testing before a purchase decision.

A rental filter press separates solids from liquids for temporary dewatering, sludge handling, wastewater clarification, and slurry management projects.

Sizing

Sizing depends on slurry volume, solids content, cycle time, desired cake dryness, and cycles needed per day. See the full sizing guide for details. View Sizing Guide →

PacPress rental filter presses are rated by cubic feet of filtration capacity. A specialist confirms the appropriate capacity and current equipment availability for each project.

Pricing

Rental pricing is quoted for each project based on press capacity, rental term, freight, support equipment, and site requirements. View Pricing →

Logistics

Delivery timing depends on equipment availability, project location, freight coordination, and site readiness. PacPress confirms the schedule during the quote process.

Service availability depends on the project location and freight requirements. Contact PacPress with the site address to confirm coverage.

Support Equipment

A rental configuration may include pumps, tanks, hoses, or related equipment when required. The project quote identifies the selected equipment and what is included.

Most projects need the press, a feed pump, hoses, a power source, space for setup, and a method for removing solids after each cycle.
