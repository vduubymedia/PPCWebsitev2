PacPress provides filter press rentals for food and beverage producers that need temporary solids separation or process wastewater clarification. A rental filter press can support seasonal production runs, plant capacity limits, and pilot testing without a permanent capital purchase.

Seasonal and Variable Production Loads

Wineries, canneries, and beverage plants often see production volume swing sharply by season, harvest, or run schedule. Process wastewater containing organic solids can exceed the capacity of permanent dewatering equipment during peak periods.

A rental filter press adds solids separation capacity during those peak windows, and can also be used to pilot test a dewatering process before committing to permanent equipment.

When Renting Makes Sense

01

Harvest or seasonal production

03

What We Need From You

Information Needed to Size and Quote This Rental

Share what you know — PacPress can help fill in gaps during the quote conversation.

  • Material or slurry type — what is being dewatered or clarified.
  • Flow rate or volume — how much material needs processing, and how often.
  • Solids percentage — if known, helps size press capacity and cycle time.
  • Desired cake dryness — how dry the discharged solids need to be for handling or disposal.
  • Project timeline — expected start date and rental duration.
  • Site location — for freight planning and site-specific requirements.
  • Available utilities — power and water access on site.
  • Support equipment needs — whether pumps, tanks, or hoses are needed alongside the press.
  • Discharge or reuse goal — where clarified water or filter cake will go after processing.
