The Washout Pit Problem

Ready-mix plants and concrete contractors generate wash water from washing out mixer drums, chutes, and equipment. That water carries cement fines and returned concrete solids, and it is highly alkaline. Left untreated, washout pits fill up fast, hauling costs climb, and compliance pressure builds around discharge and reuse.

A rental filter press separates the cement solids from the wash water on site, producing a dry filter cake and clarified water that can often be reused or discharged under applicable requirements.

Common Rental Applications

Typical ways PacPress rental filter presses are used in concrete and cement washout.

Washout Pit Dewatering

Removing accumulated cement solids from active washout pits to restore capacity.

Wash Water Clarification

Clarifying high-pH wash water from drum, chute, and equipment washdown.

Returned Concrete Processing

Dewatering slurry from returned or reclaimed concrete before disposal or reuse.

Water Reuse Support

Producing clarified water that can be considered for reuse in batching, subject to plant requirements.

Hauling Reduction

Converting wet washout solids into a drier filter cake to reduce the volume and cost of hauling.

What We Need From You

Information Needed to Size and Quote This Rental

Share what you know — PacPress can help fill in gaps during the quote conversation.

  • Material or slurry type — what is being dewatered or clarified.
  • Flow rate or volume — how much material needs processing, and how often.
  • Solids percentage — if known, helps size press capacity and cycle time.
  • Desired cake dryness — how dry the discharged solids need to be for handling or disposal.
  • Project timeline — expected start date and rental duration.
  • Site location — for freight planning and site-specific requirements.
  • Available utilities — power and water access on site.
  • Support equipment needs — whether pumps, tanks, or hoses are needed alongside the press.
  • Discharge or reuse goal — where clarified water or filter cake will go after processing.
