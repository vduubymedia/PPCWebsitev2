Direct Answer

PacPress provides filter press rentals for environmental remediation projects that need project-based dewatering and solids separation. A rental filter press can support site cleanup timelines, mobilization schedules, and discharge preparation without requiring a permanent equipment purchase.

Project-Based Timelines

Remediation projects run on defined timelines set by regulatory requirements, contract schedules, or site conditions. Water and sludge generated during cleanup often need to be dewatered before discharge, hauling, or disposal, and mobilizing owned equipment for a single project rarely makes financial sense.

A rental filter press gives contractors dewatering capacity that matches the project timeline, then demobilizes when the work is complete.

PacPress does not make specific contaminant-removal claims. Confirm site-specific treatment goals and applicable requirements with your environmental engineer or regulator.

What We Need From You

Information Needed to Size and Quote This Rental

Share what you know — PacPress can help fill in gaps during the quote conversation.

  • Material or slurry type — what is being dewatered or clarified.
  • Flow rate or volume — how much material needs processing, and how often.
  • Solids percentage — if known, helps size press capacity and cycle time.
  • Desired cake dryness — how dry the discharged solids need to be for handling or disposal.
  • Project timeline — expected start date and rental duration.
  • Site location — for freight planning and site-specific requirements.
  • Available utilities — power and water access on site.
  • Support equipment needs — whether pumps, tanks, or hoses are needed alongside the press.
  • Discharge or reuse goal — where clarified water or filter cake will go after processing.
